Common Questions About Linux Distros

Which Linux distro is best for beginners?

Ubuntu LTS, Linux Mint, and Zorin OS are often recommended for beginners because they are easy to install, well documented, and have strong community support.

Which Linux distro is good for gaming?

Pop!_OS, Garuda Linux, and Nobara Linux are popular among gamers due to better driver support and gaming-focused tweaks.
